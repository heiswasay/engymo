FAQ

Questions owners ask us

Still unsure? Ask us on the free call. There’s no pressure to sign up.

What is Engymo?

Engymo is a digital marketing agency that works only with gyms, fitness studios, and karate, martial arts, MMA, boxing and Taekwondo academies, with clients worldwide. We run Facebook, Instagram and Google ads, build gym management and accounting systems, and handle photography, videography, websites and social media for training businesses.

Do you only work with gyms and martial arts schools?

Yes. Engymo works only with gyms, fitness studios and training centres such as karate, martial arts and Taekwondo academies.

What services does Engymo offer?

Marketing is our core: Meta and Instagram ads, Google Ads and Google Maps, lead follow-up on WhatsApp and monthly reporting. We also build gym management and accounting systems, and provide photography, videography, websites and social media content. You can start with one service.

Can you set up a management or accounting system for my gym?

Yes. We set up systems for memberships, attendance, fee reminders, expenses, salaries and profit reports, for a single branch or several.

How soon will I get new enquiries?

Most campaigns start bringing enquiries within the first few weeks of going live. On the strategy call we tell you what to expect for your area.

How can my gym get more members?

Start with what people check before they visit: a complete Google Business Profile with photos, timings and reviews, so you appear for “gym near me”. Then run local Facebook and Instagram ads with a clear offer such as a free trial class, reply to every enquiry on WhatsApp within minutes, and post real videos of your classes and trainers. Engymo sets up and runs all of this for you.

How do you market a karate, martial arts or Taekwondo academy?

We plan around the academy calendar: admissions, summer camps, belt gradings and tournaments. Ads reach parents and adults near the academy with a trial class offer, and reels from classes and gradings build trust. We also set up fee reminders and attendance tracking, so new students stay.

How much should a gym spend on Facebook and Instagram ads?

It depends on your area, your fees and how many new members you want each month. Most gyms start with a small test budget, check the cost per enquiry, then put more behind what works. We recommend a monthly budget for your gym on the free strategy call, and you pay it directly to Meta.

Can you help my gym show up on Google Maps for “gym near me”?

Yes. We set up and optimise your Google Business Profile with the right categories, photos, timings and services, help you collect reviews from members, and can run Google Ads for searches like “gym near me” and “karate classes near me”.

Why choose a gym marketing agency over a general agency?

A general agency has to learn your business first. We only work with training businesses, so we already plan around trial classes, admission fees, belt gradings, kids batches, ladies-only slots and Ramadan timings, and we know which offers bring people through the door.

How much does it cost?

Every gym is different, so we price each plan around your goals, the services you need and the number of branches. You pay a monthly fee in advance, plus your ad budget, which you pay directly to Meta or Google so you always see where it goes. Message us on WhatsApp for a quote, and we’ll recommend an ad budget that fits your gym on the free call.

Do I have to sign a long contract?

No long lock-in. Most marketing plans run for 6 months, because that’s how long it takes to test, learn and grow properly. We hold a review meeting at month 3 to check what’s working and adjust. One-off work such as a website, a photo shoot or a system setup is quoted as a single project.

Do you work with gyms in my country?

Yes. Engymo works with gyms and martial arts schools worldwide. Ads, SEO, websites, software, content and reporting are all delivered remotely, and calls are booked at a time that suits your time zone. Our team is based in Karachi, Pakistan, where we shoot photos and videos in person. Elsewhere, shoots can be arranged on request, or we plan and edit content from the footage you send us.
