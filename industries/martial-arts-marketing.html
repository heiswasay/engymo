Two audiences: parents and adults

Most martial arts schools serve two very different groups. Parents want confidence, discipline, fitness and a safe environment for their child. Adults want fitness, skill, stress relief or competition. Each needs its own message, offer and ads. Mixing them usually means neither responds well.

Plan around the martial arts calendar

Admissions after school holidays, summer camps, belt gradings, competitions and new-year fitness resolutions all create moments when people are ready to start. We plan campaigns and content around these dates, so your marketing pushes when demand is highest.

Keep the students you win

Retention is where martial arts schools make their money. Attendance tracking, grading milestones and fee reminders, handled in a management system, help you spot students drifting away before they quit.

FAQ

Your questions answered

Still unsure? Ask us on the free call or message us on WhatsApp.

How do I get more students for my martial arts school?

Run separate campaigns for kids (aimed at parents) and adults, with a free or low-cost trial class. Show real classes and coaches in your ads, rank on Google Maps with steady reviews, reply to enquiries quickly, and follow up after the trial.

What is the best marketing for an MMA or BJJ gym?

Short videos of real training, coach credibility, a clear beginner-friendly trial offer, and strong Google Maps visibility. Many adults are nervous about starting combat sports, so content that shows a welcoming beginner class works well.

Do you work with martial arts schools worldwide?

Yes. We work with martial arts schools in any country. Ads, SEO, content and systems are delivered remotely.

Do you market kids’ martial arts programmes?

Yes. Kids’ programmes are often a school’s biggest earner. We create parent-focused campaigns built around confidence, discipline and safety.
