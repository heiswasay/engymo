Gym accounting that shows your real profit

A gym accounting system records every fee collected and every expense paid, so you always know what your gym actually makes. Engymo sets up simple accounting for gyms and martial arts schools, covering fees, expenses, salaries and profit reports, for one branch or many.

Why gyms need their own accounting setup

Gym income is mostly small, recurring payments from many members, plus joining fees, personal training and merchandise. Costs include rent, salaries, equipment, utilities and marketing. Without a system, it’s easy to know how much cash came in but not what the gym actually made after costs, or which branch carries the others.

Works with your management system

Fees collected in your gym management system can feed straight into accounting, so you don’t record the same payment twice. The result is one source of truth for members and money.

Not a replacement for your accountant

Our accounting system gives you clear day-to-day records and management reports. For tax filing and statutory accounts, your accountant can work directly from these records, which usually saves time and cost at year end.

FAQ

Accounting system: your questions answered

Still unsure? Ask us on the free call or message us on WhatsApp.

What should gym accounting software track?

At minimum: member fees and joining fees, personal training and other income, rent, salaries, utilities, equipment, marketing and other expenses, and a monthly profit report. Multi-branch gyms should also see each branch separately.

Can it show profit per branch?

Yes. Each branch’s income and expenses are recorded separately, with a combined report for the owner.

Does it replace my accountant?

No. It gives you accurate daily records and management reports. Your accountant can use these records for tax and statutory filings.

Can my staff use it?

Yes. Staff can record fees and expenses with limited access, while the owner sees all reports.
