Facebook and Instagram ads vs Google Ads for gyms

Facebook and Instagram are best for creating demand. You reach people nearby who weren’t searching for a gym today, and a strong video or offer gets them interested. They work well for trial classes, new-year pushes, kids’ batches and opening a new branch.

Google Ads are best for capturing demand. Someone searching “gym near me” or “karate classes for kids” is already looking, so you pay to appear at the top at the right moment. Most gyms get the best results using both: social to fill the pipeline, Google to win the people ready to buy.

What a good gym ad includes

A real face, ideally a coach or member, in the first second. A clear offer and who it’s for. Proof that the gym is real and friendly: classes, equipment, results. And one simple action, like “Book your free trial on WhatsApp”. Stock photos and generic slogans tend to cost more per enquiry because they look like every other gym.

Why follow-up matters as much as the ad

Most lost members are lost after they enquire, not before. If a reply takes hours, people move on. We set up quick replies, trial reminders and follow-ups on WhatsApp, and we track every lead through to a visit, so you can see which ads bring members and not just messages. FAQ

Gym ads: your questions answered

Still unsure? Ask us on the free call or message us on WhatsApp.

Do Facebook ads work for gyms?

Yes, when they’re built around a clear local offer and followed up quickly. Facebook and Instagram let you reach people near your gym by location and age, and short videos of real classes tend to perform well. The ad itself is only half the job. Fast replies to enquiries decide how many become members.

What is a good offer for gym ads?

A low-risk first step works best: a free trial class, a first-week pass, a body assessment or a discounted joining fee for a limited time. The offer should be simple to explain in one line and easy to claim on WhatsApp or a short form.

Should my gym use Google Ads?

If people in your area search for gyms or classes on Google, yes. Google Ads put you at the top for searches like “gym near me”, which come from people ready to join. It pairs well with a strong Google Business Profile and good reviews.

Who pays for the ad budget?

You do, directly to Meta or Google, so you always see where the money goes. Our management fee is separate and paid monthly in advance.

How long before ads bring enquiries?

Campaigns usually start bringing enquiries soon after they go live. The first few weeks are for testing offers and creatives, and results improve as budget moves to what works.
