Why gyms lose money without a system

Missed renewals, forgotten fees and members who quietly stop coming are the most common leaks in a gym’s income. When records live in a register, a spreadsheet and someone’s phone, nobody sees them in time. A management system shows who’s due, who hasn’t paid and who hasn’t visited, so your team can act while the member is still reachable.

Built for martial arts schools too

Martial arts schools have needs most gym software ignores: kids’ and adult batches, belt or grading records, monthly fees per student, and parents as the paying contact. We set the system up around how your school actually runs, and pair it with an accounting system if you want your income and expenses in one place.

Retention starts with attendance

Members who stop showing up are the ones who don’t renew. With attendance tracked, you can see when a regular hasn’t visited in two weeks and send a friendly message or a trainer check-in. Small, timely nudges like this are one of the cheapest ways to keep members longer.

FAQ

Management software: your questions answered

Still unsure? Ask us on the free call or message us on WhatsApp.

What is gym management software?

Gym management software is a system for running a gym’s daily operations: member records, plans and renewals, attendance, fee collection and reminders, class and trainer schedules, and reports for the owner.

Can you set it up for a martial arts school?

Yes. We set up systems for karate, Taekwondo and other martial arts schools, including batches, monthly student fees, parent contacts and grading records.

Can it handle more than one branch?

Yes. Each branch can be managed separately, with a combined view for the owner across all branches.

Can you move our existing member data?

Yes. We import your current member list from registers, spreadsheets or your old system as part of setup.

Do you provide training and support?

Yes. We train your front desk and trainers, and support you after launch with fixes and changes.
