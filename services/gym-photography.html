Why real photos beat stock images

People want to see what training with you actually looks like: the space, the coaches, the people. Stock photos look like every other gym and can even create doubt. Real, well-shot photos of your gym make your ads, website and Google profile more convincing, and give your social pages months of content.

FAQ

Photography: your questions answered

Still unsure? Ask us on the free call or message us on WhatsApp.

What photos does a gym need?

A wide shot of the main training area, close-ups of key equipment, classes in action, coach portraits, members training, the reception and any special areas such as a ladies’ section, a ring or a mat area. These cover your website, Google profile, ads and social posts.

How should we prepare for a gym photo shoot?

Clean and tidy the space, schedule a busy class during the shoot, ask members for consent to be photographed, and have coaches in branded kit. We send a checklist before the day.

Do you shoot martial arts schools?

Yes. We shoot karate, Taekwondo, MMA, BJJ and boxing classes, gradings and competitions.

Do you shoot outside Karachi?

Shoots outside Karachi can be arranged on request. We can also direct a shoot remotely and edit photos taken by your team.
