How gyms rank on Google Maps

Google ranks local results on three broad factors: relevance (does your profile match the search?), distance (how close are you to the searcher?) and prominence (how well known and well reviewed are you?). You can’t move your building, but you can make your profile complete and accurate, choose the right categories, add real photos, collect reviews steadily and keep your website consistent with your profile.

Website SEO for gyms

Your website should clearly say what you offer and where: one page per programme, such as strength training, kids’ karate or ladies-only classes, with your timings, prices or “from” prices, and an easy way to book a trial. Fast loading on phones, clear headings, location details and structured data all help Google understand and trust the site. We build this into every gym website we make.

Getting found in AI search (AEO and GEO)

More people now ask ChatGPT, Gemini or Perplexity for recommendations, like “best Taekwondo school for kids near me”. These tools favour businesses with clear, consistent facts across the web: a complete Google profile, an informative website, direct answers to common questions and genuine reviews. Answer engine optimisation (AEO) and generative engine optimisation (GEO) build on the same foundations as good local SEO, with extra focus on clear FAQs and structured data.

FAQ

Gym SEO: your questions answered

Still unsure? Ask us on the free call or message us on WhatsApp.

How can my gym rank higher for “gym near me”?

Complete and verify your Google Business Profile, choose the most accurate primary category, add real photos and your timings, collect reviews from members every month and reply to them, and make sure your website lists the same name, address and phone number. A fast website with a page for each programme also helps.

How long does gym SEO take?

Fixing your Google profile can show results within weeks. Website rankings and review growth build over months. SEO compounds, so the gyms that keep at it steadily usually pull ahead.

Do reviews really affect gym rankings?

Yes. The number, frequency and quality of reviews influence local rankings, and they strongly affect whether people choose you once they see you. Replying to reviews also shows people you care.

What is AEO and GEO for a gym?

Answer engine optimisation (AEO) and generative engine optimisation (GEO) mean making your gym easy for AI assistants and AI search to understand and recommend. That comes from clear facts, direct answers to common questions, structured data and a strong reputation online.
