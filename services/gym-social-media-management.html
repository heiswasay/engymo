What to post on a gym’s social media

A healthy gym feed mixes five kinds of content: proof (member stories, results), people (coach intros, behind the scenes), education (tips, technique, myths), community (events, gradings, celebrations) and offers (trials, seasonal deals). Too many offers and people tune out. Too few and nobody acts.

Consistency beats virality

Most gyms don’t need viral posts. They need pages that look alive and trustworthy when a potential member checks them after seeing an ad or a Google listing. Posting steadily, every week, with real photos and reels, does that.

FAQ

Social media: your questions answered

Still unsure? Ask us on the free call or message us on WhatsApp.

How often should a gym post on Instagram?

Consistency matters more than volume. Three to five quality posts or reels a week, plus regular stories, keeps most gym pages active and visible.

What content works best for gyms?

Short reels of real classes, coach tips, member stories, behind-the-scenes moments and clear offers. Real people and real sessions outperform stock images.

Do you manage TikTok and LinkedIn too?

Yes. We plan and publish for Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and LinkedIn, depending on where your members are.

Do I approve posts before they go live?

Yes. We share the monthly calendar for approval before scheduling.
