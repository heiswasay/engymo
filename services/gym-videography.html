What makes a gym reel work

The first second decides everything. Strong gym reels open with movement or a face, show one clear idea such as a class, a transformation story or a coach’s tip, and end with a simple next step. Captions matter because most people watch without sound. Keep them short, and let your real people carry the video.

Video for ads vs video for your feed

Feed content builds trust over time, while ad videos have one job: get a qualified person to enquire. We edit the same footage both ways, so one shoot day can supply your ad campaigns and your social media for weeks.

FAQ

Videography & reels: your questions answered

Still unsure? Ask us on the free call or message us on WhatsApp.

What videos should a gym post?

Class highlights, coach introductions and tips, member stories, behind-the-scenes moments, event coverage, and clear offer videos such as a free trial invitation. A mix of trust-building and offer content works best.

How long should a gym reel be?

Most perform best at 7 to 30 seconds. Lead with the strongest moment, keep one idea per reel, and add captions.

Can you edit footage we film ourselves?

Yes. We can send a shot list and filming tips, then edit your footage into reels and ads.

Do you film gradings and tournaments?

Yes. We cover belt gradings, tournaments, summer camps and other events for martial arts schools and gyms.
