What every gym website needs

Visitors look for the same things: what you offer, where you are, your timings, how much it costs, what the space looks like, who the coaches are, and how to start. Put those on the site clearly and add a WhatsApp or booking button that’s always visible. Real photos and short videos do more than any slogan.

Built to rank

Every site we build includes the foundations of gym SEO: clean page structure, fast loading, location details, structured data for your business and FAQs, and pages for each programme. That gives you the best chance to appear for searches like “gym near me” and in AI search answers.

Landing pages for ads

Sending ad traffic to a homepage usually wastes money. A focused landing page with one offer, one message and one button converts better. We build these for each campaign we run.

FAQ

Websites: your questions answered

Still unsure? Ask us on the free call or message us on WhatsApp.

What should a gym website include?

Your programmes and classes, timings, fees or starting prices, location and map, photos of the space, coach profiles, member reviews, FAQs, and a clear way to book a trial on WhatsApp or by form.

Should my gym show prices on the website?

Showing prices, or at least a starting price, usually brings better-qualified enquiries and saves your team time. If prices vary, show a “from” price and what’s included.

Is the website SEO-friendly?

Yes. Every site includes fast loading, clean structure, structured data, location details and FAQs, which help with Google and AI search.

Can you build a website for a martial arts school?

Yes. We build sites for karate, Taekwondo, MMA, BJJ and boxing schools, including pages for kids’ and adult programmes, timetables and gradings.
